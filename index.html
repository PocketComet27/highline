Highline Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

Highline is a one-tap stacking game for Android, made by PocketComet ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. If you have a question, write to pocketcomet27@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your phone. We have no servers and receive none of it.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads.
  • "Remove ads" is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your information.

1. Information saved on your phone

To keep your game going between sessions, Highline saves these on your device:

  • your best scores and records, coins, unlocked themes, missions, daily challenge and streak progress;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, difficulty, theme in use) and which tips you have already seen;
  • whether you own "Remove ads", as a copy of what Google Play reports, so the game works offline;
  • simple counters that space out ads, such as when the last ad was shown.

This information stays on your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not included in Android's cloud backup. Uninstalling Highline, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

Highline shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: a banner on the menu screens, an occasional full-screen ad between runs, and optional video ads you choose to watch for a reward (such as a second chance or extra coins).

When ads are loaded, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following with Google:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (city or region, not your exact location);
  • Interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • Diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the ads, such as launch time and hang rate;
  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

Google uses this data to show ads, to measure them, and to prevent fraud and abuse. It is sent encrypted (TLS). Ads are limited to content suitable for teens. How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Highline asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is loaded. You can change your answer at any time under Settings → Privacy options in the game.

Your ad choices. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by phone). You can also manage Google ad personalisation at My Ad Center.

Buying "Remove ads" turns off the banner and the full-screen ads. The optional reward videos stay available, so the AdMob SDK still runs while you play.

3. In-app purchase (Google Play Billing)

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own terms and the Google Privacy Policy. The game only asks Google Play whether you own "Remove ads", and it keeps the answer on your phone. We never receive your card, bank or billing details.

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to check your purchase with Google Play.
  • Vibration: for small haptic taps when a block lands. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Google Play Billing: to buy and restore "Remove ads".
  • Advertising ID: added by the AdMob SDK for the ads described above.

Highline does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, files, photos or GPS location.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not run our own servers, accounts or analytics, so we do not hold any personal information about you.
  • We do not sell personal information or share it for money.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

6. Children

Highline is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and you want Google's ad data about that device removed, reset the advertising ID on that device and contact us; we will help you reach Google.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, UK data protection law, California's privacy laws or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we hold no personal information, there is nothing we can send or delete on our side: the data on your phone is yours to delete by uninstalling the game. Ad data is held by Google, and you can use the choices in section 2 or Google's privacy controls. If you need help, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information in a new way, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version of the game is released.
